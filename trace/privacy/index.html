TRACE

Privacy policy · testing version · updated October 28, 2026

Trace turns where you go into a piece of art. Your location history stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Nobody else can see it, including the person who makes Trace.

What Trace records

  • Your location, in the background, while you move: position, time, accuracy, speed, direction and altitude.
  • Motion activity from your iPhone (walking, cycling, driving), to tell how you were traveling.
  • Battery level and location-use statistics from iOS, to measure how much battery Trace uses.
  • Missed-trip reports, when you tap the button saying a trip wasn't recorded.

Where it goes

  • Your iPhone. Everything above is stored on your phone.
  • Your own iCloud. Trace backs up your recordings to the private iCloud storage of your own Apple account, so you can restore them on a new phone. Only you can read it. You can turn this off for Trace in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud.
  • Exports you choose to make. Trace can export your data as a file. It goes wherever you send it, and nowhere else.

Trace has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your data is never sold or shared.

Street maps

To fit your paths to streets, Trace downloads map data from OpenStreetMap through the public Overpass service (overpass-api.de). When that service isn't working, Trace uses a free copy of the same service run by Kumi Systems (overpass.kumi.systems). Each request asks for one square of map about 1 km across. Like visiting any map website, the service sees your internet address and the map square requested. It never receives your trips, your times or your location history.

Testing through TestFlight

While Trace is being tested, it's installed through Apple's TestFlight. Apple shares with the developer what TestFlight normally shares: your name and email if you were invited, when you installed, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. See Apple's TestFlight terms for details.

Deleting your data

  • In Trace's Settings you can delete a single day, a stretch of time, or everything. Deleting also removes it from your iCloud backup.
  • Private zones stop Trace from recording at places you choose, and can erase what was already recorded there.
  • Deleting the app removes everything on your iPhone. To remove the backup too, delete Trace's data in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage.
